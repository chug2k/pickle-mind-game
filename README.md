# Pickle: Mind Game

Play ten doubles pickleball decisions at your own pace. Tap a shot and its target or use keys 1–4. Each choice has a court trace and an explanation. You can replay any ball before advancing. [Play the drill](https://clpanic.com/pickle-mind-game/)

This is the owner’s static release of the May 2026 game. The original shot-and-aim decisions supply the grades. The coaching mode adds situation cues, explanations, and a first-attempt scorecard. The timed 3D rally is available from the menu. [Play the timed rally](https://clpanic.com/pickle-mind-game/rally.html)

The court and ball artwork are drawn by the game. Third-party library notices accompany the build. [Software credits](./third-party-licenses.txt)
